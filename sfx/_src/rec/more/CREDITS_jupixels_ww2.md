**CC0** sounds need no credit. **CC-BY** sounds may be used in any project, including commercial ones, but you must credit
them: name the author, the original title, a link to it, and the license (CC BY 3.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/,
CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), and say that the sound was edited (trimmed). A line in the game's
README or credits screen is enough. `sounds.json` carries `license`, `author` and `source_url` on every one of these entries.
