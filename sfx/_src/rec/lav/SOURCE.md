# rec/lav

All files here come from the CC0 collection https://github.com/lavenderdotpet/CC0-Public-Domain-Sounds
(Kenney packs, Warfork CC0 assets, OpenGameArt CC0 packs, and the "bb" / "Micro Pack" CC0 recordings).
Each source pack states CC0 1.0 (see LICENSE_CC0.txt and warfork_assets_cc0.txt).
WAV originals were converted to OGG (q6) and capped at 75 s to keep the repository small.

Prefixes: wf_ Warfork, bang_ 25 CC0 bang SFX, bfh1_ 75 CC0 breaking/falling/hit, wet_ water/slime SFX,
wm_ 100 CC0 wood/metal SFX, knife_ Kitchen Knives, woosh_ Organic Wooshes, mech_ Smol Mechanisms,
tool_ Toolbox Rummaging, em_ NazdyNate Electromagnetic Sounds, fan_ Fans and Drones, loop_ 30 CC0 SFX loops,
scifi_ Kenney Sci-fi Sounds, ui_ Kenney Interface Sounds.
